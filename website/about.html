About The Project

About Leaf

Leaf helps people understand where their money went, where it is going, and what they need to prepare for next.

Product Overview

Leaf is an offline-first personal finance platform built natively for Android. Rather than functioning simply as a retrospective expense tracker, Leaf bridges past financial activity with future obligations: helping users understand where their money went, where it is going, and what they need to prepare for next.

From scheduled payment reminders (EMIs, credit cards, rent, utility bills, subscriptions) and a 14-day upcoming dues horizon, to monthly category budgets, milestone savings goals, an interactive financial payment calendar, shared group expense splitting, and precision financial calculators — Leaf delivers complete financial clarity in a fast, private, distraction-free environment.

Many modern personal finance apps require cloud registrations, mandate continuous internet connectivity, monetize telemetry data, or clutter screens with aggressive loan offers and partner ads. Leaf takes the opposite approach: 100% offline-first architecture, local SQLite encryption, zero tracking, and complete user ownership of their financial records.

Engineering & Design Principles

Leaf is engineered natively for Android with a commitment to mathematical precision, privacy, and architectural elegance:

  • iPhone-Quality Polish & Floating Capsule Dock: Elevated floating capsule navigation dock with spring-animated sliding pill physics, translucent luminous glassmorphism, tactile haptic confirmations, and edge-to-edge content bleed with 34dp resting clearance.
  • Soulful Notification Audio Chime & Brand Icon: A crafted 2-note musical alert tone (leaf_chime.wav) paired with the official brand monochrome status bar glyph (ic_stat_leaf.xml) for instantly recognizable, pleasant financial alerts.
  • Native Android Architecture: Built purely with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Room DB for fluid 60fps performance across 5 core navigation hubs (Dashboard, Transactions, Split, Planning, Insights).
  • Proactive Payment Reminders: Precision scheduling for EMIs, credit cards, utility bills, rent, insurance, and subscriptions with exact Android AlarmManager alerts, configurable reminder times/days, and month-end recurrence preservation.
  • Upcoming Obligations Horizon: Dedicated 14-day cash horizon on the Dashboard highlighting imminent payments, complete with instant 1-tap "Mark Paid" logging directly into expenses.
  • Financial Payment Calendar: Integrated monthly calendar mapping historical daily spending alongside scheduled future obligations side-by-side with color-coded day indicators.
  • Unified 3-Tab Planning Hub: Cohesive management of monthly category budgets (with 80% & 100% threshold indicators), milestone savings goals with contribution logs, and scheduled reminders in one unified screen.
  • Insights & Financial Ledger: Month-to-month surplus carry-forward ledger, month-over-month (MoM) financial comparisons, and a smooth cubic Bézier spending trends chart.
  • Settings 2.0 Control Center: Comprehensive preference management with granular notification toggles, biometric app lock, currency formatting, multi-language support (English, Hindi, Marathi), and in-app update checks.
  • 6 Precision Calculators: Built-in calculators for Loan EMI, SIP Wealth Accumulation, Fixed Deposit (FD), Recurring Deposit (RD), Discount & Taxes, and GST.
  • Split & Shared Groups: Subunit integer division for shared expenses, shared finance groups, "Who Owes Whom" net balance calculation with automated debt offsetting, UPI QR attachments, and WhatsApp payment reminders.
  • Google Drive Backup v2: Direct, optional connection to your private Google Drive covering all app entities with zero intermediary servers and backward-compatible v1 restores.
