About The Project

About Leaf

Leaf helps people understand where their money went, where it is going, and what they need to prepare for next.

Product Overview

Leaf is an offline-first personal finance platform built natively for Android. Rather than functioning simply as a retrospective expense tracker, Leaf bridges past financial activity with future obligations: helping users understand where their money went, where it is going, and what they need to prepare for next.

From scheduled payment reminders (EMIs, credit cards, rent, utility bills, subscriptions) and a 14-day upcoming dues horizon, to monthly category budgets, milestone savings goals, an interactive financial payment calendar, shared group expense splitting, and precision financial calculators — Leaf delivers complete financial clarity in a fast, private, distraction-free environment.

Many modern personal finance apps require cloud registrations, mandate continuous internet connectivity, monetize telemetry data, or clutter screens with aggressive loan offers and partner ads. Leaf takes the opposite approach: 100% offline-first architecture, secure local storage, zero tracking, and complete user ownership of their financial records.

Design & Product Principles

Leaf is engineered with a commitment to mathematical precision, privacy, and calm user experience:

  • iPhone-Quality Polish & Floating Capsule Dock: Elevated floating capsule navigation dock with smooth motion, pleasant tactile feedback, and a clean edge-to-edge layout.
  • Pleasant Audio & Handcrafted Details: A crafted musical chime paired with Leaf's signature status bar icon and dashboard footer branding ("Crafted with 💚 in India" / "Vinay") linking directly to this About section.
  • Fast, Native Performance: Built with modern native architecture for instant, responsive performance across all screens (Dashboard, Transactions, Split, Planning, Insights).
  • Flexible Balance Controls & Dual Ledgers: Switch seamlessly between Combined Balance (Account + Cash), Account Balance only, or Cash Balance only with a 1-tap privacy eye toggle and persistent mode memory.
  • Proactive Payment Reminders: Never miss EMIs, credit cards, utility bills, rent, or subscriptions with reliable on-time reminders and recurrence support.
  • Upcoming Obligations Horizon: A dedicated 14-day cash horizon on the Dashboard highlighting imminent payments with instant 1-tap logging.
  • Financial Payment Calendar: Integrated monthly calendar mapping historical daily spending alongside scheduled future bills side-by-side.
  • Unified Planning Hub: Cohesive management of monthly category budgets, milestone savings goals, and scheduled bills in one unified screen.
  • Interactive Insights & Drill-Downs: Understand where your money goes with tap-to-filter category breakdowns, month-over-month comparisons, and interactive spending trend curves.
  • Redesigned Settings & Control Center: Apple-inspired grouped section cards organizing privacy screen masking, biometric app lock, flexible currency formatting, backups, and localization.
  • Three Dedicated Home Widgets: Account Balance Widget with privacy toggle, Financial Overview Widget with balance, quick actions & budget progress, and Quick Actions Widget for instant logging.
  • 6 Precision Calculators: Built-in calculators for Loan EMI, SIP Wealth Accumulation, Fixed Deposit, Recurring Deposit, Discounts, and Taxes.
  • Split 2.0 with QR Image Editor: Equal splits or itemized bill sharing for meals and trips, integrated bill calculator, crop/zoom/rotate QR image editor, and instant WhatsApp summary sharing.
  • Flexible Data Backups: Direct, optional connection to your private Google Drive or local storage files with zero intermediary servers.
