Core Principles

Built for clarity, privacy and reliability

Personal finance software should help you manage your money without complicating your life.

100% Offline-First

All your transactions, custom categories, and balances stay safely on your device. Track spending anywhere without needing internet access or online accounts.

Cash & Account Tracking

Keep your cash-in-hand spending separate from bank account transactions with configurable default sources for seamless everyday record keeping.

Exact Financial Math

Built with precision arithmetic, guaranteeing zero rounding errors or discrepancies across your monthly ledgers, totals, and exported reports.

Fast Recording

Track every expense with effortless clarity

Adding and editing expenses and income is fast and intuitive. Choose between Cash and Account sources, adjust transaction date and time with precision, type amounts with automated comma formatting, and use Quick Add for frictionless transaction entry on the go.

  • Independent default sources for Income and Expenses
  • Edit transaction date and time with native Material time picker
  • Quick Add dialog with auto-selected categories
  • Instant 1-tap Undo protection for deleted entries

Planning Hub

Set budgets and reach your savings goals

Take control of future finances with Leaf's 4th primary tab: Planning. Define overall and category monthly spending budgets with real-time threshold warnings, create milestone-based savings goals with dedicated contribution tracking, and archive completed goals with a tap.

  • Monthly & Category Budgets: Track spending limits with dynamic 80% caution and 100% exceeded indicators
  • Savings Goals with Milestones: Target amounts, target dates, and real-time progress calculations
  • Contribution History: Add funds directly to goals with a dedicated contribution audit trail
  • Lifecycle Management: Archive finished targets to keep your active list focused and clean

Insights & Trends

Visual analytics and month-over-month trends

Understand where your money goes with Leaf's 5th primary tab: Insights. Inspect month-over-month spending trends, visualize spending velocity with high-precision cubic Bézier curve charts, and review carry-forward opening balance ledgers.

  • Smooth Bézier Trends Chart: Interactive visual curve plotting daily cumulative outflow trajectory
  • Month-over-Month (MoM) Analytics: Instant percentage comparison with preceding month spending velocity
  • Monthly Continuity Ledger: Seamless opening balance carry-forward and net savings breakdown
  • Native PDF Export: Generate bank-grade statements with running balance directly on-device

6 built-in financial calculators for instant clarity

Plan loans, investments, taxes, and shopping savings without installing extra calculation utilities. Leaf includes six precision financial calculators with real-time feedback and clean result summaries.

  • Loan EMI Calculator: Monthly installments, principal vs interest split, and total repayment

Split & Groups

Split shared expenses & organize group finances

Whether splitting a casual lunch or managing an ongoing trip with friends, Leaf's dedicated Split tab makes group finance effortless. Create Shared Finance Groups, calculate "Who Owes Whom" net balances with automated debt offsetting, attach recipient UPI QR codes, and dispatch WhatsApp payment reminders.

  • Shared Finance Groups: Group expenses by trip, flatmates, or events with dedicated group totals
  • "Who Owes Whom" Net Balances: Directional debt offsetting computes the fewest possible settlement transactions
  • UPI QR & WhatsApp Reminders: Attach payment QR codes and generate 1-tap WhatsApp reminder messages
  • Optional Ledger Sync: Choose whether shared bills record into your primary account ledger or remain isolated

Data Ownership

Your data, backed up your way

Leaf v1.0.8 features Google Drive Backup v2. Back up your transactions, recurring schedules, custom categories, monthly budgets, savings goals, contributions, and split groups directly to your personal Google Drive with complete backward compatibility.

  • Full Schema v2 Coverage: Backs up all v1.0.8 entities including Budgets, Savings Goals, and Split Groups
  • Backward-Compatible Restores: Effortlessly restores legacy v1 backups alongside modern v2 backups
  • Private & Guarded: Saved inside your private Google Drive app folder with explicit restore confirmation
