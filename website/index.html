Split 2.0 • Bill Splitting

Real-world custom bill splitting, in-app calculator & smart WhatsApp sharing

Splitting dining and group expenses in real life isn't just dividing a single sum equally. Leaf features Split 2.0 — built around an intuitive "Base Equal Share + Item Responsibility" model. Itemize dishes and drinks, assign individual or shared consumers, calculate amounts with an integrated in-app calculator, select friends via the device contact picker, and dispatch context-rich WhatsApp payment reminders directly to recipient chats.

  • Item-Level Custom Splitting: Itemize restaurant dishes, drinks, or tickets with explicit item costs. Assign single or multiple consumers per item with optional custom allocations.
  • Base Equal Share Foundation: Common appetizers, taxes, tips, and unallocated amounts automatically distribute equally across all participants with zero double-counting.
  • Integrated Financial Calculator: Access an instant keypad calculator directly on bill amounts and custom allocations with 1-tap value insertion.
  • Device Contact Picker & Direct WhatsApp Chat: Rapidly add friends with 1 tap from device contacts and launch WhatsApp reminders directly into their individual chat.
  • "Who Owes Whom" Net Balances: Directional debt offsetting computes the fewest possible settlement transactions across multi-person expenses.

Data Ownership

Your data, backed up your way

Leaf features Google Drive Backup v2. Back up your transactions, recurring schedules, payment reminders, custom categories, monthly budgets, savings goals, contributions, and split groups directly to your personal Google Drive with complete backward compatibility.

  • Full Schema Coverage: Backs up all Leaf entities including Reminders, Budgets, Savings Goals, and Split Groups
  • Backward-Compatible Restores: Effortlessly restores legacy v1 backups alongside modern v2 backups
  • Private & Guarded: Saved inside your private Google Drive app folder with explicit restore confirmation
